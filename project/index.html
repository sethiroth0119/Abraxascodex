What is Abraxas Codex

A Studio and a Community, in One Place

Abraxas Codex is the engine Hidn Studios uses to design, iterate, and publish Mythic Spellbook — and it's the window into that process for every player who cares about the world of Abraxas.

Every card you play was designed in the Card Forge. Every character was written in the Lore Codex. Every faction emerged from debates in Threads. The platform that builds the game is the same one you log into — because we believe great games are made alongside the people who love them.

Living Canon

Every card, character, faction, and world event lives here — updated in real time as the game develops.

Community Feedback Loop

Players post directly to developers in Threads. Bug reports go straight into the dev pipeline. Your input ships in the next patch.

AI-Powered Creation

Athena AI knows every rule, faction, and character in Abraxas. The Sprite Forge generates game-ready art from lore descriptions.
